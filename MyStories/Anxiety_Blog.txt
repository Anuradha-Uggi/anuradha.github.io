Things often do not happen the way we expect. This is one common situation that triggers anxiety. It reached an extent where everything started to feel never interesting, and every small problem started to seem so big. 
Not everything can be shared with friends and family. Especially, in my case, certain outcomes when they turn out to be unexpectedly negative or ignorance of the people in crucial times trigger extreme anxiety and make me feel purposeless. 
I have no clue how to overcome and simply detach from what's happening. As it's perfectly said in the Gita, only actions are in our control and never their fruits. Very true, but following seems to be hard. Every time this happens, I feel like fighting a war against myself. 
On one hand, I try to convince myself that anything that happens in life is temporary, no matter what. Whether it's success, failure, happy, or sad. These emotions last for a shorter period. Yet, I knowingly fight to overcome negative emotions. On the other hand,
showing up myself problem-free and happy to the loved ones around me is another great war keeps going. While I ponder over these random thoughts of how to attain self-realization and keep myself cool and calm all the time without too much 
concern about what happens next, this came to my mind: why can't I express my feelings in words and get rid of the fear of failure and anxiety? Here are my uncontrollable, random thoughts that put me under stress and make me weak. 

1. Personal life: Relationship, the biggest question mark. For many years, I was never concerned about it. Now, the time has come. Many doubts and questions started to flood. Questions that always put me in confusion: Is it compulsory for me to marry? Yeah, maybe the right person 
can add strength? What if I end up in the wrong hands? How to decide? And I have no definitive answers to these questions. The next immediate question blinks. Why can't I simply give up on this? But I don't want to. 
2. Professional life: There is a whole ocean of interesting and long-standing problems to be addressed. Very exciting. I am a lifetime indebted to God for where I have reached today despite countless odds. Never ever imagined that I would reach here one day.
Everything feels like a miracle. Never imagined that I would dare to argue with my parents and convince them about my higher education aspirations. When they started finding matches for me, I thought I would end up becoming a typical woman with household tasks, giving up on my dreams of leading an independent life. 

The next step is to wrap up my research here and take up a job.
